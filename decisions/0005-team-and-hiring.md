# ADR-0005: No hiring pre-traction; solo + informal help only

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-03-21

## Context

Early SilkPanda work is exploration: shop interviews, manual onboarding, and a narrow MVP. Hiring before traction adds burn and coordination cost.

## Decision

**No employees or contractors until traction signals** (e.g. multiple active shops, repeatable onboarding). Builder-led development with occasional informal help (friends, shop owners for feedback) only.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Hire junior dev early:** Faster code, slower product learning and focus.
- **Agency build:** Loses iteration speed with shop owners in the room.

## Consequences

- Scope must stay ruthlessly small (ADR-0006)
- Onboarding may be high-touch and manual — acceptable for pilots
- Revisit hiring when support load or build velocity blocks growth
